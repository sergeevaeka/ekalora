About Ekaterina

Where Technology, Art & Natural Beauty Meet

I am Ekaterina Sergeeva — a commercial model, tech entrepreneur, and experienced event organizer, bringing together the worlds of fashion, innovation, art, and business.

With a Master’s Degree in Engineering and over 15 years of experience in engineering, IT, and technology leadership, my professional journey spans the automotive and pharmaceutical industries, industrial automation, digital payment solutions, and emerging technologies.

Today, I am the founder of The Miracle Tree, an AI-powered technology company, where I combine my technical expertise with a passion for innovation, artificial intelligence, and Web3 technologies.

Beyond engineering and entrepreneurship, I have extensive experience in public speaking, conference organization, brand presentations, and corporate events. Having led teams responsible for technical communications, digital presence, UI/UX, product quality, exhibitions, and professional workshops, I understand how to bring people, brands, and ideas together. I have also organized conferences in the beauty and technology industries and coordinated exhibition stands and brand experiences.

Alongside my business career, I pursue my passion for commercial modeling, contemporary art, fashion, and athletics. As a model in the 30+ category, I embrace 100% natural beauty, with no plastic surgery, healthy glowing skin, and an athletic physique shaped by an active lifestyle and consistent fitness training.

My combination of natural elegance, technical intelligence, entrepreneurial experience, and confident on-camera presence allows me to represent brands with authenticity and sophistication. I am particularly interested in collaborations involving fine jewelry, luxury watches, automotive brands, innovative technology, premium lifestyle, beauty, and fitness & wellness.

Whether in front of the camera, presenting a technology innovation, or organizing a high-profile event, I bring the same qualities: professionalism, creativity, attention to detail, and a genuine passion for connecting people and ideas.
